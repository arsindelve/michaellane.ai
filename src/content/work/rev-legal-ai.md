## Find what wins your case

That was Rev's promise to lawyers, in four words. Their pitch went on: long hearings, dozens of calls, hours of interviews. Legal teams were drowning in recordings, and Rev's job was to turn them into evidence they could search, cite and use in court. On G2, Rev put it even shorter: "Cut hours of review down to minutes."

Rev built its business on accurate transcription. The legal assistant builds on top of it, applying LLMs to the recordings and transcripts legal teams already work with. My job was to lead the organization that built it: set technical direction, structure the teams, and make delivery predictable while the technology changed under us month to month.

## Every stage of a case

Rev's own headline for the product was "Making every stage of your case more efficient." In their words, and in the order a case happens:

- **Case intake and consultation:** tailored prompts that surface the key patterns in an interview "without compromising the original record," because in legal, nuance is everything.
- **Depositions, investigations and discovery:** upload testimony and interviews, and AI insights "surface contradictions and key statements across multiple files."
- **Multi-File Case Intelligence:** audio depositions, video interviews, written records and documents in one searchable workspace, flagging discrepancies across every format.
- **Motions and pre-trial:** summaries of testimony, with key evidence clipped and shared straight from the transcript.
- **Trial:** real-time transcription in the courtroom, so a lawyer can cross-examine and impeach a witness without missing a word.

Their tagline for all of it: "Transcribe, cite, win."

## How I led it

- **Reliability first.** In legal work a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer. Retrieval, evaluation and guardrails were treated as product features, not afterthoughts. Every answer had to point back to the source.
- **Secure by default.** Evidence is the most sensitive data a firm holds. The product shipped SOC 2 audited, GDPR and PCI compliant, with no third-party data sharing.
- **Clarity and ownership.** Engineers knew what we were building, why, and who owned what, so they could move with urgency.
- **Applied AI that earns its place.** We used LLMs, retrieval and agent workflows where they made the product measurably better, not because they were available.

## Number one

In Summer 2025, G2 ranked Rev the number one Leader in AI Legal Assistant software, ahead of 76 other products, based entirely on customer reviews. G2's own category page named Rev the Leader, the Easiest to Use and the Top Trending product, all three, and is blunt about how it ranks: "We do not allow paid placements."

By Fall 2025, Rev held six G2 badges, including Leader and Momentum Leader, and a 4.7 out of 5 from 557 reviews. Rankings like that come from lawyers using the product every day, in hearings and depositions, and telling other lawyers it works.
