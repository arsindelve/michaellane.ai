## The domain

ToolBox Solutions built hosted category-management and point-of-sale analytics for CPG manufacturers and major retailers—aggregating daily, weekly, and monthly POS, shipment, inventory, syndicated, and consumer-panel feeds into executive dashboards and custom analytical reporting (the platform **SPS Commerce** later acquired).

## What we built

I led two .NET teams owning the **Hosted BI Dashboards** and **Custom Analytics** products:

- **Massive SQL Server ETL:** Engineered high-volume ingestion and transformation pipelines using dozens of stacked CTEs and 1,500-line stored procedures to reconcile messy, multi-source retail data.
- **Penny-exact reconciliation:** In retail and financial reporting, one penny off is as bad as \$1,000 off—if the totals don’t tie out to the exact cent, customers stop trusting the dashboard. We built strict validation and reconciliation into every rollup.
- **Hosted BI, cubes & MDX:** Delivered interactive web dashboards and custom category insights used daily by CPG brand teams and retail buyers—even pushing into OLAP cubes and MDX as analytical scale grew.

That was my data era—by the end of that run, I could write complex SQL in my sleep.
