## The situation

Kobo’s e-commerce experience was split across three separate storefronts: mobile, web, and e-reader. That meant three codebases to maintain and three places to fix every bug—slowing down product work across a global catalog of three million e-books.

## What we built

I led three teams (12 developers) through a ground-up rewrite of the e-book e-commerce solution to ship **One Store**—a single, unified storefront for buying e-books that worked across every device:

- **One Store architecture:** Consolidated mobile, web, and e-reader purchasing into one shared storefront codebase instead of three parallel stacks.
- **New search:** Rebuilt search across Kobo’s three-million-title catalog so readers could find books quickly from any device.
- **New PDP:** Shipped a redesigned Product Detail Page as the centerpiece of browsing and purchasing.
- **“Recommended for you”:** Built personalized merchandising powered by data from Kobo’s big data team.
- **Christmas-morning peak load:** Kept the storefront healthy through Kobo’s biggest traffic spike of the year—when millions of newly unboxed e-readers hit the store at once (a holiday season that saw ~150% YoY E Ink eReader growth, millions of new December registrations, and 22M+ pages read on Christmas Day 2012).
